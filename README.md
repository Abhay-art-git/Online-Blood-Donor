# 🩸 Online Blood Donor Website

## 📘 Overview
The **Online Blood Donor Website** is a web-based platform designed to **connect blood donors with recipients** efficiently and securely.  
It allows users to **register as donors**, **search for blood donors based on blood type and location**, and **request blood in emergencies**. The system ensures quick response times and supports the critical goal of saving lives.

This platform can be used by hospitals, NGOs, or individuals to **facilitate blood donation campaigns and emergencies**.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Create a platform for blood donors and recipients to connect.  
- Allow users to search for donors based on **blood group, city, and availability**.  
- Maintain a database of registered donors for quick access during emergencies.  
- Provide a secure and user-friendly interface for all users.  

---

## 🧩 Features
- ✅ **User Registration & Login** – Donors and recipients can create accounts.  
- ✅ **Donor Database** – Store information like name, blood type, location, and contact.  
- ✅ **Search & Filter** – Find donors based on blood group, city, and availability.  
- ✅ **Request Blood** – Recipients can send blood requests to matching donors.  
- ✅ **Admin Dashboard** – Manage users, requests, and campaigns.  
- ✅ **Email Notifications** – Notify donors when their blood is requested.  
- ✅ **Responsive Design** – Accessible on desktops, tablets, and mobile devices.

---

## 🧪 Workflow
1. **User Registration** – Donor or recipient creates an account.  
2. **Profile Management** – Donors update availability and contact information.  
3. **Search Donors** – Recipients search for compatible donors.  
4. **Blood Request** – Recipients send request notifications to selected donors.  
5. **Admin Oversight** – Admin manages donor database and monitors requests.
